TrafficSync in real cities

Every traffic light is a TrafficSync agent that sees only its own approaches and talks to its neighbours. Each replay shows the same vehicles under the city's own signal program and under all our agent designs (actuated, max-pressure, CoMP, CoCycle) side by side, on the real street map. Delays are averages over the replayed 10 minutes (same seed); the multi-seed results are in results/SUMO/summary.md.
